Podcasts have been around for a very long time. In fact, they’ve been around since before I was born, in the form of Radio Shows that is. In a similar fashion to other types of media that found a way to the internet, podcasts did too, and soon enough became a niche.

There are podcasts for every topic out there. From widespread topics such as Tech and Film to the even more niche audience based podcasts such as nature. Heck, there’s even a podcast about podcasts.

Myself along with many people in the tech industry have been listening to podcasts for a long long time, but ask anyone who doesn’t use the internet as religiously as some of us do what a podcast is, and they’re bound to be confused. However, in 2014, Serial was launched, and with it came a glimmer of hope for the podcast world, a hope that podcasts would go mainstream.

There are many reasons why podcasts are so amazing. One of them being that you can find a podcast on just about any topic you can think of, and have instant access to opinions and news relating to said topic. The main reason as I see it is, podcasts don’t restrict you. With movies and TV shows, you have to have your eyes glued to a screen to be immersed in the content. A similar sentiment goes for reading. With podcasts however, you can listen to them in bed, in the shower, on your daily commute, while at the gym. Especially with the advent of high production quality podcasts such as Serial and Gimlet Media’s Startup, podcasts are becoming more and more intriguing and immersive.

## **Here are a few of my favourites, to get you started.**

So now maybe you want to start listening to podcasts, but you’re not sure where to get started. Below I’ve listed out some of my favourite podcasts. Apple has a decent enough native app for downloading and listening to podcasts, but I’d personally recommend [Pocket Casts](http://pocketcasts.com/). It’s available on Android, iOS and the web, and offers a lot of great search and discovery features as well as a sync feature that I can’t live without. It is on the pricier side however.

## **Serial**

This show was all the rave of 2014. It follows a reporter as she looks into a cold case to find out if the alleged murderer was rightfully imprisoned. This show got my dad, a person who didn’t care at all for podcasts, to start listening. It’s an amazing show.

## **StartUp**

This show follows Alex Blumberg of This American Life fame as he started his own venture / foray into the podcast world. Sort of like a BTS into the StartUp industry. You should also check out their other new show, Reply-All.

## **Upvoted**

Upvoted follows Alexis Ohanian as he looks into the most upvoted posts on Reddit and the inspiring stories behind them.
