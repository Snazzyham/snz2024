Hey, I'm Soham. I started off my career on the operations side of tech consulting. I then went to school for a bit, didn't do much studying but learned a lot. Followed that up by spending over 5 years at [GOODSTUPH](https://goodstuph.org) making dope ideas come to life, before moving into the [Blockchain and Gaming](https://chillchat.com/) space for a bit.

Now, life has come full circle and I'm back on the operations side of tech consulting. I'm one of the co-founders at both [Otterdev](https://otterdev.io) and [Forge](https://toolsbyforge.com). 

At Otterdev, we focus on helping marketing teams build high performance and stable CMS-driven websites, making sure teams have confidence in the ability that they can update their website without needing developer intervention. 

At Forge, our goal is to help small businesses run more efficiently by building custom software solutions and aiding in conscious AI adoption, without the worry of paying perpetual license fees to a big SaaS provider that doesn't take their needs into consideration.

Across my career, I've had the pleasure of working with some great people from Netflix, MediaTek, HBO, Porsche and more, and my work has received recognition from the likes of Clio, The Webby Awards, Citra Pariwara and Gong.
