To launch season 3 of HBO's Westworld, which heavily revolved around data privacy, we created an app to make users think twice about the kind of data they post on line. We created an API scraper that looked through public data available on Instagram to craft assumptions regarding their personality.

The program looked at multiple touch points such as tagged users, comments replied to, locations tagged and made use of Google's Vision API to triangulate who their friends were, where they spent their time and some of their most recent travel destinations. We then provided these findings to the user in as eerie a format as we could.
